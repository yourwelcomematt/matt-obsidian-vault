### Movement: 4R36

## Precautions

Avoid the following places for wearing or keeping the watch:
- Places where volatile agents (cosmetics such as polish remover, bug repellent, thinners etc.) are vaporising
- Places where the temperature drops below 5°C or rises above 35°C for a long time
- Places affected by strong magnetism or static electricity
- Places affected by strong vibrations
- Places of high humidity
- Dusty places

Maintaining water resistance
- Do not pour running water directly from a faucet onto the watch
- Do not turn, pull out, or push back in the crown when the watch is wet
- Do not leave moisture, sweat and dirt on the watch for a long time - wipe it off with a soft cloth
- Do not wear the watch while taking a bath or a sauna
- After soaking the watch in seawater, wash it in clean pure water and carefully wipe it dry
	- Put some water into a bowl first, and then soak the watch in the water to wash it

## Operation

Starting the watch from a stopped state
- Slowly turn the crown 20 turns clockwise (will not start the watch immediately), or swing it from side to side until the seconds hand starts moving (will start the watch sooner)
- Turning the crown further than 20 turns will not break the spring
- Once the watch is wound up fully, it operates for about 41 hours (power reserve)
- If the watch is used without being wound up fully, gain or loss of time may result
	- To avoid this, wear the watch for more than 10 hours a day
	- If the watch is used without being worn on the wrist, be sure to wind it up fully every day at a fixed time

Setting the date and day of the week
- Do not set the date or day of the week between 9:00 p.m. and 4:00 a.m., as doing so can damage the automated date-change mechanism which is engaged during this time
	- If you're setting up the watch for the first time or after a long time and it displays a time during this period, move the hands forward until you're out of the danger zone before setting the date
	- https://www.reddit.com/r/Watches/comments/23iskt/lesson_on_mechanics_or_why_you_shouldnt/
- Pull the crown out to the first position
- Turn it clockwise to set the day of the week
	- Can choose between English and Kanji - https://cotoacademy.com/days-of-the-week-in-japanese-guide/
	- The language you didn't choose will be displayed between 1:00 a.m. and 4:00 a.m.
- Turn it anti-clockwise to set the date
- The date will automatically change at around 12 o’clock midnight, and the day of the week will automatically change at around 4:00 a.m.

Setting the time
- Ensure the day and date are set to yesterday
- Pull the crown out to the second position when the seconds hand is at 12 to stop it
- Turn it clockwise to advance the hands until the date changes over to the current date, then advance to the current time - this ensures we know whether we are in A.M. or P.M.
- Turn it anti-clockwise to go back if needed
- Be sure to turn back the minute hand a little behind the desired time and then advance it to the exact time
- Push the crown back to the normal position in accordance with an accurate time source to restart the watch

Adjusting the date at the end of the month
- Required at the end of February and 30-day months, as the watch will otherwise automatically change to the 29th or 31st respectively
- On the morning of the 1st of the new month, pull the crown out to the first position
- Turn it anti-clockwise to set the date to "1"
- Push the crown back to the normal position

Adjusting the band
- Allow a little clearance with your wrist to ensure proper airflow - leave enough room to insert a finger between the band and your wrist

## Daily Care

### Accuracy

- The accuracy of mechanical watches is indicated by the “daily rate"
	- For the 4R36 movement, this is +45 to -35 seconds per day when worn on the wrist in a temperature range of 5°C to 35°C
	- In real-world use, the 4R36 often performs much better than this
- Accuracy can vary due to:
	- The position of the watch
	- The length of time the watch is worn on the wrist
	- Whether the mainspring is fully wound or not
	- Temperature (tends to lose time at high temperatures and gain time at low temperatures)
- In order to improve accuracy, it is important to regularly supply energy to the balance that controls the speed of the gears
	- This is because as the mainspring unwinds, the force weakens, resulting in a loss of consistency
	- Relatively steady accuracy can be obtained by wearing the watch on the wrist frequently
- If you're not happy with the accuracy, you can take it to get regulated
	- Can achieve + or - a few seconds per day

### Crown

- In order to prevent corrosion of the crown, turn it from time to time

### Magnetism

- Keep the watch more than 5 cm away from magnetic products, as they may cause the watch to temporarily gain or lose time, or stop operating
	- This is because the balance spring may be influenced by a strong magnetic field
	- If the watch becomes magnetised and its accuracy deteriorates to an extent exceeding the specified rate under normal use, the watch needs to be demagnetised

### Bands

Metallic band (bracelet)
- Moisture, sweat or soil will cause rust even on a stainless steel band if they are left for a long time - wipe it off with a soft cloth as soon as possible
- To clean the soil around the joint gaps of the band, wipe it out in water and then brush it off with a soft toothbrush - ensure the watch body is protected from splashes
- Watch out for rusted pins or pins that are poking out

Leather band
- Susceptible to discolouration and deterioration from moisture, sweat and direct sunlight
- Wipe off moisture and sweat as soon as possible by gently blotting them up with a dry cloth
- Do not expose the watch to direct sunlight for a long time
- Refrain from wearing it while bathing, swimming, and working with water (Aqua Free bands are the exception)

Polyurethane band
- Susceptible to discolouration from light, and may be deteriorated by solvent or atmospheric humidity
- Translucent, white, or pale coloured bands can easily adsorb other colours (sticks to the outer surface), resulting in colour smears or discolouration
- Wash out dirt in water and clean it off with a dry cloth
- When the band becomes less flexible, replace it with a new one - if you continue to use it, it may develop cracks or become brittle over time

 Silicone band
 - Easily dirtied, and may become stained and discoloured
 - Wipe off dirt with a wet cloth or cleaning tissue
 - Cracks may result in the band being cut
