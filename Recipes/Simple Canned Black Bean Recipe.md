## Ingredients

- 1 can black beans
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 large onion, diced
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1 jalapeno or other chilli, diced
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp salt

## Method

1) Heat saucepan on medium-high heat
2) Add olive oil, onion, tomato, jalapeno, garlic, cumin, oregano, and paprika
3) Stir and saute for 5 min (until onions are translucent and soft)
4) Add black beans (DO NOT DRAIN)
5) Stir and let simmer until it reaches your desired consistency (can add water or broth if you want it soupier)
6) Remove from heat (will continue to thicken), add salt, and serve
