- I love pushing the Kodak-made Fuji 400, I often push it 1 stop but a friend of mine always does 2 stops. It looks beautiful at 800 but at 1600 you really have to watch your highlights.
- I pushed Fuji 400 to 800 and it looked great IMO. Portra 800 looks good only 1 stop push, after that it falls apart quickly, and honestly Portra doesn’t look good under tungsten light, too many color corrections are needed. Vision 3 500t pushed well up to 3 stops I think. Cinestill 800t pushes good as well but probably only 2 stops realistically.
