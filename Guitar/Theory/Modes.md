## Major Modes

### Lydian

- Raised 4th degree
- Turns the ii chord into a major chord
	- Used in a I, II, IV, I chord progression (e.g., Eight Days a Week), however we immediately return to the standard major scale with the regular IV chord)

### Mixolydian

- Flattened 7th degree
- Results in a flat VII chord

## Minor Modes

### Dorian

- Raised 6th degree
- Gives a more light and whimsical feel
- Turns the iv chord into a major chord
	- Used in a i, III, VII, IV chord progression (e.g., Mad World)

### Harmonic Minor

- Raised 7th degree
- Turns the v chord into a major chord
