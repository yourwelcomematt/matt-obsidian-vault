### SMC Pentax-M 75-150mm F4

https://www.pentaxforums.com/lensreviews/SMC-Pentax-M-75-150mm-F4-Zoom-Lens.html
https://www.reddit.com/r/pentax/comments/14qfjdr/opinions_on_75150mm_f4_smcm_lens/
https://www.pentaxforums.com/forums/10-pentax-slr-lens-discussion/282758-smc-pentax-m-75-150-vs-smc-m-135-3-5-a.html
https://www.pentaxforums.com/forums/10-pentax-slr-lens-discussion/476442-looking-old-lenses-zooms-35-105mm-3-5-m-75-210mm-4-constant-plus-m-200mm-4-a.html

https://www.pentaxforums.com/forums/10-pentax-slr-lens-discussion/320143-best-sub-100-telephoto-lens.html
- The best telephoto zoom lens I've ever run across under $100 is the M 75-150 f4. It's better than the F 70-210 in the 100 to 150 range, and it comes with a built-in hood, which is kind of handy. The best telephoto prime I've ever used is the aforementioned M 135 f3.5.
- While it might be difficult to find a F 70-210 for under $100, you can pay less for the Takumar F 70-210 (most samples have a good multi-coating similar to SMC from the A-series) that is optically and physically similar to the ones with the Pentax label. I feel it is optically better than the very good m75-150 that remains in possession. And that 75-150 is at least as good as the m135 f/3.5 (long gone from my collection)

https://forum.mflenses.com/what-is-your-experience-of-the-pentax-m-75-150mm-t62511.html
- For whatever it's worth, I like it better than the outstanding Nikon Series E 75-150mm 3.5. While the Nikon may be sharper, the Pentax is better built, has better colors and more pleasing bokeh. This is all on film, I haven't used either lens on digital.
