## Pushing Film

- When you underexpose your film by a certain number of stops, and overdevelop to compensate
- E.g., telling the light meter in your camera that you're shooting 800 ISO film, even though you're actually shooting 400 ISO film, then overdeveloping by 1 stop

### Advantages
- Increased contrast
- Enables higher shutter speeds, allowing you to shoot lower speed film at night

### Disadvantages
- Increased grain
- Loss of shadow detail due to underexposure - you can't develop what isn't there to begin with
- Costs more to develop

### Recommended push amounts for different film stocks
- HP5 Plus - max 2 stops (e.g., 1600)
- Ultramax - max 2 stops (e.g., 1600)
- Gold - max 1 stop (e.g., 400)

## Pulling Film

- Opposite of pushing
- Main effects are reduced contrast and grain
- Costs the same
