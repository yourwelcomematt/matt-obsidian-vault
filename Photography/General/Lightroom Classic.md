## General navigation shortcuts

- Press E to view an individual photo in the Library module
	- It's faster to navigate between photos in the Library module, as the Develop module is looking at the full resolution RAW file and has to apply settings to every edited photo
- Press G to go to grid view in the Library module
- Press D to go to the Develop module

## Library module

- Used to view and organise your photos
- All your photos sit under the Catalog in the left sidebar
	- You can have more than one catalog, but can only have one open at a time

### Importing photos

- To add photos to your catalog, click the Import button
	- By default, it will open a file explorer tab in the left sidebar (Source) where you can select the folder that contains the photos you want to import
	- Alternatively, you can drag and drop your photos into LR from Finder
	- Ensure you are in Add mode so that the photos aren't moved from their original location
	- If you want to import all the photos in the folder, click "Check All"
	- If you want to import a specific photo, click the checkbox on it, or press P when it's highlighted or in the individual view; only these photos will be imported when you click Import
		- Press X to unpick a photo, or use the spacebar to toggle it
	- The process of choosing which photos to import and which to get rid of is called culling
	- The tabs in the right sidebar allow you to choose what to do with your photos once you import themjj
		- File Handling
			- Click "Add to collection" to further organise your photos into collections, which are like sub-folders
		- Apply During Import
			- Can apply presets automatically using "Develop Settings"
			- Can add metadata like a copyright


- You can view all your photos at once in the grid view

## Develop module

- Used for editing
- Press \ to see before and after
- History tab in the left sidebar shows a timeline of all the edits you've made to the photo
- Copy and Paste buttons are used to copy and paste settings from one photo to another
- Press Cmd + Shift + E to open the Export window

Basic
- Profile
- White Balance
	- Temperature
		- Controls the overall warmth of the image from blue to yellow
	- Tint
		- Controls the green/magenta tint in the image
		- Used to counteract the green tint from artificial lights
- Exposure
- Contrast
- Highlights
- Shadows
- Whites
- Blacks
- Texture
- Clarity
- Dehaze
- Vibrance
- Saturation

Tone Curve
